Website redesign research

Process & video library examples

A working reference page for the Heyday website redesign. These builders and design firms use short videos, process education, client stories, or structured resource libraries in ways worth studying.

The goal is not to copy a website. It is to identify useful patterns: sequential storytelling, simple explanations, human presence, and a clear path from curiosity to trust.

THE UP STUDIO
Modern Home Design Process
01 — strongest reference

The Up Studio

theupstudio.com/architectureprocess

A ten-part architecture and interiors process presented as a sequence. Each phase gets its own short video, real project examples, and a clear explanation of what happens next.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Make the process feel like a journey, not a FAQ.
  • Number the phases and keep the language plain.
  • Pair each video with enough written context to be useful without watching.

LAUREN HOMES
Our Building Process
02 — closest content strategy

Lauren Homes

lauren-homes.com/our-building-process

A straightforward series of short videos beginning with the first phone call and continuing through lot ownership, financing, meetings, selections, contract, construction, and completion.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Answer the questions a homeowner actually asks before hiring.
  • Start before construction — budget, lot, financing, and expectations matter.
  • Keep each topic narrow enough for a short video.

CUSTOM ONE HOMES
Building Process Series
03 — best family storytelling

Custom One Homes

customonemn.com/media/videos

A seven-part series follows one real family through the build: introductions, selections, preconstruction, groundbreaking, framing, finishes, and the final walkthrough.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Use real families to make the process emotionally understandable.
  • Let viewers see the builder and homeowner together.
  • Mix process education with the human story of the home.

RGB CUSTOM BUILDERS
New Customer Videos
04 — strongest pre-sale education

RGB Custom Builders

rgbhomes.com/new-customer-introductory-videos

Introductory videos focus on the decisions that happen before a build starts: budget, land, architectural design, bank submission, contracts, and selections.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Use videos to remove uncertainty before the sales call.
  • Teach budget and contract topics instead of hiding them.
  • Organize content around customer decisions.

COUTO HOMES
Building Process Series
05 — strong construction sequence

Couto Homes

coutohomes.com/phase-videos

An 11-part series moves from lot selection through foundation, framing, mechanicals, drywall, finishes, cabinets, countertops, paint, and closing day.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Make construction stages easy to scan.
  • Use consistent thumbnails and video lengths.
  • Give homeowners a visual roadmap of what will happen on site.

COMERIO HOMES
Build Process
06 — best meeting-by-meeting explanation

Comerio Homes

comeriohomes.com/our-build-process

Their process page explains the actual client meetings and milestones: pre-construction, design selections, pre-drywall, trim walkthrough, warranty orientation, and pre-closing.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Explain what the client is expected to do at each milestone.
  • Describe what gets decided in each meeting.
  • Turn internal process into visible reassurance.

BIJOU BUILDS
Video Library
07 — best scalable video library

Bijou Builds

bijoubuilds.com/videos

A more developed content library with filters for build process, project tours, before-and-after, behind the scenes, education, client stories, and Q&A.

What Heyday should borrow
  • Use categories once the library grows beyond roughly 20 videos.
  • Mix short social-style clips with longer explainers.
  • Keep education, projects, and client stories in one searchable place.

Direction for Heyday

Use The Up Studio for the sequential structure, Lauren Homes / RGB for plain-language customer education, and Custom One for the human, family-centered tone. The result should feel less like a marketing library and more like Hardy personally walking a homeowner through what happens next.

Suggested priority: The Up Studio → Lauren Homes → Custom One → RGB → Comerio → Couto → Bijou
