Strawberry clover

Establishing in November, with green cover through March. Flowers generally arrive May–June, beyond this timeline.

Daikon

Fast green growth · early root maturity

Root harvest: approximately November 29–December 29 (40–70 days after sowing). If left unharvested, plants may stay green and flower in spring; March flowering is tentative.

Hairy vetch

Winter foliage · spring growth surge

Slow establishment in November, then green winter growth with a spring surge. Flowers generally begin around April, beyond this timeline.
